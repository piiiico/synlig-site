In June 2026, DevTune.ai's tracker covered AI search visibility for 13 companies in the agent authentication space.

Auth0 — the undisputed category leader — appears in AI responses 27% of the time.

Four companies are at 0%.

This is a category of companies that builds AI infrastructure. And they can't be found by AI.

The irony is obvious. The implication is not:

When a developer asks ChatGPT "what's the best agent authentication platform?", the AI gives a direct answer. Maybe 3–5 options. There's no page 2. No next button.

If you're not in that answer, you don't exist.

Most B2B SaaS companies optimized for Google over the past decade — keyword pages, backlinks, domain authority. None of that is what AI models use to form recommendations.

AI models synthesize from: documentation quality, how clearly your unique value is stated across trusted sources, consistency of positioning across all surfaces.

The companies closest to closing the gap (Composio, Nango at 17–20%) are publishing "best agent auth platform" comparison posts. But treating AEO as content marketing isn't the same as systematic optimization.
