AEO agency Norway: what it is, why it matters locally, and what to expect

Answer engine optimization for Norwegian businesses is genuinely different from generic AEO. Here is what the Norwegian market looks like and where Synlig Digital fits in.

When someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity "best accountant in Bergen" or "which insurance broker in Oslo handles maritime?", the answer does not come from your website ranking in Google. It comes from a combination of structured data, Norwegian aggregator listings, your Google Business Profile, and whether AI crawlers can actually read your site.

Most Norwegian businesses have none of these things in order. When we run an AI visibility audit on a typical Norwegian SMB, it scores between 18 and 32 out of 100. The main gaps are almost always the same: missing or malformed structured data, absent from Proff.no or 1881.no, Google Business Profile incomplete, and robots.txt blocking AI crawlers.

That is the problem Synlig Digital exists to fix — and why a Norway-specific approach matters.

Why Norwegian AEO is different from generic AEO

International AEO guides focus on English-language search behavior and US/UK aggregators. Apply that playbook to Norway and you will miss the signals that actually shape Norwegian AI answers.

  • Norwegian aggregators carry weight. Finn.no, Proff.no, 1881.no, Eniro.no, and Gulesider.no are sources AI assistants draw on heavily for Norwegian business information. Missing from these = effectively invisible in Norwegian-language AI search.
  • Norwegian-language structured data is different. Schema.org markup in Norwegian needs different field conventions. The inLanguage attribute, address format, and postal codes matter for how AI systems interpret locality signals.
  • Google AI Overview in Norwegian uses different data. The Norwegian Google AI Overview draws on different sources than the English version of the same query. A Norwegian business cannot rely on English-language signals for Norwegian-language queries.
  • The competitive gap is wider. Most Norwegian businesses have done zero AEO work. Getting into the top of Norwegian AI answers requires less than it would in English — the floor is lower, so the ceiling is more reachable.

Frequently asked questions

  • What is an AEO agency?
    An AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) agency helps businesses appear in AI assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, and similar. Unlike SEO, which targets search engine rankings, AEO focuses on structured data, aggregator presence, AI crawler accessibility, and content formatted for citation. When someone asks an AI assistant about your category or location, AEO is what determines if you are mentioned.
  • Is there an AEO agency in Norway?

Contact: hei@synligdigital.no.
  • How is AEO different from SEO?
    SEO optimizes for search engine rankings — where your website appears on Google's results page. AEO optimizes for AI answers — whether your business is cited when someone asks ChatGPT "best X in Y city". The inputs are different: AEO relies on structured data, AI-readable formats, aggregator listings, and factual accuracy rather than backlinks and page speed. The two overlap but are not the same — a site with strong SEO can still score F on AEO.
  • How long does answer engine optimization take?
    Structured data and robots.txt fixes can be picked up by AI crawlers within days. Norwegian aggregator registrations propagate in 1–4 weeks. Full AI visibility improvement — moving from absent to reliably cited — typically takes 4–8 weeks after implementation. Our client results bear this out: Nordic Lithium went from F to B grade within 6 weeks of implementation.
  • Do I need AEO if I already do SEO?
    Depends on whether AI-assistant citations matter for your customer acquisition. If your customers use ChatGPT or Perplexity to research their options before buying — and most B2B buyers in Norway now do — then SEO alone is not enough. Search behavior is fragmenting: a significant share of commercial queries now go directly to AI assistants, not Google. If you are not optimizing for both, you are visible in one channel and invisible in the other.

    Yes. International companies operating in Norway need Norwegian-specific AEO work: Norwegian-language structured data, presence on Norwegian aggregators (Proff.no, 1881.no, Finn.no), and a Google Business Profile at their Norwegian address.
