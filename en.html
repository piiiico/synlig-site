What AEO actually is

AEO is the engineering side of being mentioned by AI assistants. Schema.org markup, machine-readable metadata, canonical entity definitions, consistent business identity across data sources, content that maps cleanly to how questions are asked.

It is roughly to AI assistants what SEO was to Google in 2005: a set of structural fixes that take a site from "the algorithm cannot tell what this is" to "the algorithm has enough signal to cite it confidently."

We are a small team, based in Stavanger, Norway. Most of our day-to-day is Norwegian SMB work. The methodology is language-agnostic, and we have shipped foundations for English-language SaaS clients on the same basis.

How we work

Both packages are one-off. No meetings, no dashboards, no subscription — you get a document you can forward internally, and the raw data behind it.

  • Baseline measurement. Five buying-intent questions from your market, each run at least nine times over several days with byte-identical prompts. You approve the questions first. You get how often your domain was cited with a link, and the names of who was cited instead.
  • The changes, written out. JSON-LD, citable questions and answers, llms.txt and AI-crawler rules for up to ten pages — ready to paste, or delivered as a pull request, within five business days. You publish them, and they stay in your own pages afterwards.
  • Re-measurement 30 days after you publish. The same five questions, nine new runs, before and after in one document with the raw data. Written outcome criterion: at least one citation of your domain. If it is not met, we run another round at no extra charge.
